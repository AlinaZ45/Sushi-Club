Questions & Answers

A quick guide to the reservation workflow, table assignments and daily operations.

Reservations

What should I check before confirming a reservation?
Review the reservation date, time, guest count and contact details. Check availability for the selected time and make sure suitable table capacity is available. Assign the table or tables before completing the confirmation workflow.
What does Pending mean?
The guest has submitted a request, but the team has not confirmed it yet. Review the details and availability, then confirm or decline the request. If declining, enter a clear reason when prompted.
When should I use Reconfirmed?
Use the reconfirmation workflow when a confirmed reservation has been changed and needs the guest-facing status to return to Pending for the team to confirm the updated details.
How do I handle a guest cancellation?
Open the reservation and review its current status. A cancellation submitted by the guest is shown in the reservation list. Do not delete reservation history to make a booking disappear.
When should I mark a reservation as No-show?
If the guest has not arrived within 15 minutes after the reservation time, follow the restaurant's no-show rule and update the reservation status accordingly. Check with the team before releasing a table if the situation is unclear.

Tables & availability

How do I assign tables?
Open the reservation's table-assignment control. Select one or more available tables with enough combined seats for the party, or use Auto Select. Review the selection and choose Save Tables.
What if no table combination has enough seats?
Do not confirm a reservation against insufficient capacity. Check another available time, review existing assignments, or contact the manager for guidance.
What do the Tables and Availability tabs show?
Tables shows the live table plan and configured table inventory. Availability shows time slots and whether capacity is available or blocked for new reservations.
Can I delete a table?
Only remove a table if it has no reservation history. If it has history, disable it instead so historical reservation records remain intact.

Guest communication & account

Where can I read guest messages?
Open the relevant reservation and use its message conversation to read and reply to the guest. Keep communication related to that booking in its conversation.
Where should I add an internal note?
Use the reservation's edit workflow and its staff-note field. Keep internal operational notes separate from messages intended for the guest.
What if I cannot sign in?
Check that you are using the approved staff e-mail and the correct password. Use Forgot Password to request a secure reset link. If your account is not approved or remains inactive, contact the account administrator.
How do I protect guest information?
Use guest details only for restaurant operations. Do not copy, export, share or disclose personal contact information outside approved work processes. Never share your staff password.
Important: This guide describes the current staff workflow. If a reservation, table assignment or guest request is unclear, check with the manager before changing its status.
